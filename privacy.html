Privacy policy

Hymns of Reformed Worship for Android · Last updated 3 November 2026

The short version

The app does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no adverts and no analytics, and it works without an internet connection.

What stays on your phone

Your settings (theme, text size and reading options), your favourite hymns and your list of recently opened hymns are saved only on your phone, so the app remembers them next time. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Internet and permissions

All the hymns are built into the app, so it does not need the internet. The only link out is "Buy the printed hymnbook", which opens the Tabernacle Bookshop website in your browser. That website has its own privacy policy.

The app never asks you for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files. Like most Android apps, its package lists a few standard permissions that come with the framework it is built on: internet access, vibration for button feedback, storage access on Android 12 and older, and "display over other apps". The app does not use any of them to collect or send information, and Android does not grant storage or display-over-apps access unless you allow it yourself.

This website

This page and the download page are hosted on GitHub Pages and the app file is downloaded from GitHub, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses. See GitHub's privacy statement. The pages use fonts from Google Fonts.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions can be raised on the app's GitHub issues page.
